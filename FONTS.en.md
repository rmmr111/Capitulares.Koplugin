# Recommended drop-cap fonts

Place TTF/OTF files in `.adds/koreader/fonts/Initials/`, restart KOReader, and choose **Capitulares → Customize → Initials**. No third-party fonts are bundled.

- [Mosaic Initials](https://www.dafont.com/mosaic-initials.font) — ornate mosaic initials; DaFont says “100% Free”; review terms.
- [Goudy Initialen](https://www.dafont.com/goudy-initialen.font) — traditional decorated initials; DaFont says “100% Free”; check individual licensing details.
- [Camelot Caps](https://www.dafont.com/camelot-caps.font) — fantasy initials; **personal use only** according to DaFont.
- [Arabesque Initialen](https://www.dafont.com/arabesque-initialen.font) — ornate floral initials; DaFont says “100% Free”.
- [Acorn Initials](https://www.dafont.com/acorn-initials.font) — botanical decorated initials; DaFont says “100% Free”.
- [Victorian Initials One](https://www.dafont.com/victorian-initials-.font) — Victorian decorated initials; DaFont says “100% Free”.
- [Royal Initialen](https://www.dafont.com/royal-initialen.font) — gothic ornamental initials; DaFont says “100% Free”.
- [Roycroft Initials](https://www.dafont.com/roycroft-initials.font) — Arts & Crafts initial caps; DaFont says “100% Free”.
- [Uncial Antiqua](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Uncial+Antiqua) — medieval manuscript style.
- [Cinzel Decorative](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cinzel+Decorative) — classical decorative capitals.
- [Almendra Display](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Almendra+Display) — calligraphic display.
- [MedievalSharp](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/MedievalSharp) — pointed medieval fantasy.
- [Eagle Lake](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Eagle+Lake) — fantasy manuscript.
- [Pirata One](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Pirata+One) — gothic display.

Fonts from Google Fonts are provided under open font licenses; consult the specific font license before redistribution. [OFL FAQ](https://openfontlicense.org/).
